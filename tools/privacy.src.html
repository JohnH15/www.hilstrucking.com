2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit the application form: your full name, phone number, email address, how you apply (Company Driver or Owner-Operator), your CDL-A OTR experience, the state that issued your CDL, any comments or truck details you add, and your consent choices.   • to contact you about driving opportunities with HILS and answer your questions;

The website form is a request to be contacted. It is not the formal driver application. Formal FMCSA application documents, background check disclosures and consents are provided separately during onboarding.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers who help us run the website and communicate with you, such as our website hosting provider, our email service, Google (reCAPTCHA and advertising measurement) and Meta Platforms (advertising measurement), and only as needed for those services;
  • with messaging platforms you choose to use with us (WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Instagram), under their own privacy policies;
  • when required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect our rights and safety;
  • in connection with a merger, sale or reorganization of our business.

Google's use of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Meta's use of information is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as we need it for recruiting and to meet legal and record-keeping requirements. After that we delete it or remove details that identify you.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections to the website and spam protection on the form. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing info@hilstrucking.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we may keep information that the law requires us to keep.

  • You can limit personalized advertising in your Google Ad Settings and in your Meta ad preferences, and manage cookies in your browser settings.
  • Depending on the state where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us and we will respond as required by applicable law.

10. Children
